Amplify Inspirations Media

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. Who we are

Amplify Inspirations Media ("we", "us") produces and publishes short-form quiz videos to social media platforms, including YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. This policy explains what data our publishing software handles, and what it does not.

You can reach us at amplify.inspirations.media@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers our internal publishing software — the tools we use to upload our own videos to our own social media accounts — and this website.

Our software is not a product offered to the public. Nobody signs in to it, creates an account with it, or uses it to publish their own content. It is operated by us, on our own infrastructure, to publish our own videos to accounts we own and control.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, sell, rent or share:

  • Email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses of any third party
  • Payment or financial information
  • Location data, device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Biometric data, contacts, photos or files from any user's device

The one exception is the public comments left on our own videos, which we read in order to reply to them. That is described in full in section 5.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and does not profile visitors. It is a set of static pages.

4. Platform data we access, and why

To publish a video, our software connects to social media platforms using their official APIs. Access is granted by us, for our own accounts, through each platform's standard authorisation screen. What we access is limited to what publishing requires:

Platform What we access Why
YouTube Permission to upload videos to our channel; our own channel's public statistics; public comments on our own videos To publish episodes, to see how our own videos performed, and to read and reply to comments
Instagram Permission to publish content to our own professional account; that account's basic profile; public comments on our own posts; insights for our own account and posts To publish Reels and Stories, to read and reply to comments, and to see how our own posts performed
TikTok Basic profile information for our own account; permission to upload or post videos; public statistics for our own videos To send episodes to our account, to confirm which account we are posting as, and to see how our own videos performed

We request the narrowest permissions that allow publishing to work. We do not request access to any other person's account, to direct messages, or to follower lists. The only comments we can read are those left publicly on our own videos.

5. Comments on our videos

We read the public comments left on our own videos and posts so that we can reply to them. We do this on the platforms whose APIs support it — currently YouTube and Instagram. To do this we access, for each comment on something we published:

  • The commenter's public display name
  • The text of the comment
  • Its like count and the date it was posted
  • Whether our channel has already replied in that thread

All of this is information the platform already displays publicly beneath the post. We do not access commenters' email addresses, subscriber lists, or any part of their account beyond the public comment itself.

How we use it

Solely to read and reply to comments under our own videos. A reply is drafted by a person and posted only after that specific reply has been explicitly approved — nothing is posted automatically.

We do not:

  • Build profiles of commenters, or track individuals across videos
  • Use comments for advertising, targeting or audience segmentation
  • Use comments to train machine learning or AI models
  • Sell, rent, publish or otherwise disclose comment data to anyone
  • Contact commenters anywhere other than in a public reply to their comment

Storage and retention

Comments we have fetched are stored in a working file on our own systems, so that drafted replies are not lost between sessions. This file is private, is not published, and is kept only as long as it is useful for replying. If a commenter deletes their comment, or a platform removes it, we delete our copy on our next refresh of that video's comments.

If you have commented on one of our videos and want our copy of your comment deleted, email amplify.inspirations.media@gmail.com and we will remove it. Deleting your comment on the platform itself also removes it from public view immediately, which is the faster route.

6. Performance statistics

We read the performance statistics each platform reports for our own posts and accounts — such as views, likes, comment counts, shares, watch time and follower counts. These figures describe how our own content performed. They are aggregate numbers supplied by the platform and do not identify individual viewers, and we do not attempt to link them to any individual.

7. Access tokens and how they are stored

When we authorise our software, each platform issues an access token — a credential that lets the software act on our own account. These tokens:

  • Are stored on our own computers and servers, in files readable only by the operating system account that runs the software
  • Are never committed to source control, shared with third parties, or transmitted anywhere other than back to the platform that issued them
  • Are used only to perform the publishing actions described above

Tokens expire on a schedule set by each platform and are refreshed automatically, or replaced by repeating the authorisation.

8. Sharing with third parties

We do not share data with third parties. The only external services our software communicates with are the social media platforms themselves (YouTube, Instagram and TikTok) and the hosting provider that stores our own video files for upload. Each platform's own privacy policy governs what it does with the content we publish:

  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Instagram (Meta) Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

9. Retention

Access tokens are kept for as long as we continue publishing to the account in question, and are deleted when we stop using it or when access is revoked. Comment data is retained as described in section 5. Video files and the records of what we published are kept as part of our own production archive.

10. Revoking access

Because the only accounts involved are our own, access can be withdrawn by us at any time from each platform's settings:

  • Google: Third-party apps with account access
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

Revoking access immediately stops our software from being able to publish to that account.

11. Children

Our software is not directed to children, and it collects no information from anyone, including children. Our videos are published on platforms that require users to meet their own minimum age requirements.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to amplify.inspirations.media@gmail.com.
