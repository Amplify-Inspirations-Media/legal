Short-form quiz videos, made and published in-house.

YouTube · Instagram · TikTok

Amplify Inspirations Media writes, produces and publishes short-form quiz videos — episodic series that ask viewers a handful of questions about the shows and subjects they already love, and give them a reason to argue about the answers.

What we do

  • Write the episodes. Every question, answer and distractor is researched and authored by us.
  • Produce the videos. Episodes are rendered in-house from our own templates, artwork and motion design.
  • Publish them. Our own software uploads finished episodes to our own accounts using each platform's official API.
  • Reply to viewers. We read the comments under our videos and answer them, with every reply written and approved by a person.

Our publishing software

The tooling that uploads our episodes is internal. It is not a product, it is not offered to the public, and nobody signs in to it. It acts only on social media accounts we own and control, and it does no automated engagement — it posts no unapproved comments, sends no messages and follows no accounts.

What it accesses on each platform, and how that data is handled, is set out in full in our Privacy Policy.
